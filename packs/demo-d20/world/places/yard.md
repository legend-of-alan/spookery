Wagon ruts cross the mud. Briar leans on his staff at the gate, and a mill cart with a loose wheel sits across the lane. A ladder goes up to a loft, and a shed stands off the yard. The lamps get lit at dusk. Nell is waiting on the lane for her flour, past the gate and the cart. Briar opens the gate to someone who bests him. The cart rolls once its wheel is warded with iron or braced.
